About Coach TJ

Tyler Jacobsen · Former college baseball player · Algonquin, IL

I played high school baseball at Jacobs High School, where I was named All-Fox Valley Conference as a senior. After two seasons at Kishwaukee College, I transferred to Mississippi Christian University (D2), where I played two more seasons and earned my degree in accounting.

Having competed at every level, I know what it takes to succeed. My lessons focus on fundamentals, confidence, and the right mindset, so athletes can reach their full potential on and off the field.
