Send Your Video

How to Film

  • Two angles: one from the side facing the player's chest, one from straight behind.
  • Phone at hip height, about 10–15 feet away, held sideways and steady (a tripod or fence works).
  • Use slow-motion if your phone has it.
  • Whole body in frame, from head to feet, for the full rep.
  • 3–5 reps per angle (tee, front toss, or live), or 3–5 catching reps: receiving, blocking, throws.

Ready? Upload Your Video

Upload straight from your phone, no account needed. In the file name or note, include the player's name, age, hitting or catching, and which package you want. I'll text back to confirm and send payment details before I start your breakdown.
