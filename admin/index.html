Breakdown Checklist

  1. Check Stripe → Payments for a new purchase or plan.
  2. Find their video in Dropbox.
  3. Record your voice-over breakdown (OnForm or screen recording).
  4. Send the breakdown and 2–3 drills by text or email.
  5. Delete or move the video in Dropbox to free up space.
