Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

TJ Baseball Group LLC (“TJ Baseball,” “we,” “us”) provides baseball hitting and catching lessons and remote video coaching led by Coach TJ (Tyler Jacobsen). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you give us, such as a parent or player's name, phone number, and email address. This includes information submitted through our Facebook and Instagram lead forms, our booking calendar, text messages, and email.
  • Player information such as the player's name, age or grade, position, and training goals.
  • Videos you upload or send to us for remote coaching.
  • Booking details, like the lesson type, date, and time you choose.
  • Payment information, which is collected and processed by Stripe. We do not see or store full card numbers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and contact you about lessons or remote coaching, including by phone call or text message.
  • To schedule lessons, send confirmations and reminders, and provide coaching and video breakdowns.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To occasionally share news or offers about TJ Baseball. You can ask us to stop at any time.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run TJ Baseball, and only as needed:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), when you submit one of our lead forms
  • Cal.com, for booking and scheduling
  • Stripe, for payments
  • Dropbox, for receiving coaching videos

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Videos and Player Privacy

Videos and player information are used only to coach that player. We will never post a player's video or name publicly without a parent's permission. For players under 18, we ask that a parent or guardian provide information and make purchases.

Keeping and Deleting Information

We keep information only as long as needed to provide coaching and keep basic business records. Uploaded videos are deleted after coaching is complete unless you ask us to keep them. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

Text Messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your inquiry, lessons, or coaching. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages.
